FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before we start.

The practical details behind services, pricing and project delivery.

What does OrbitIT do?+

We provide web and ecommerce development, interface design, search and digital marketing support, creative content and IT consultancy. Each engagement has a defined scope agreed with the client.

Are the listed prices monthly subscriptions?+

No. All 13 listed packages are one-off services or content batches with specific limits. Ongoing work and subscriptions require a separate written agreement.

What currency do you use?+

All published package prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). We confirm the scope, any applicable taxes and third-party charges in a written quotation before an agreement is made.

Can I buy a package immediately on the website?+

This website accepts enquiries, not online payments or automatic orders. We first confirm the requirements, deliverables, total fees and delivery timetable.

What do you need from me to start?+

Usually your objectives, existing brand files, approved copy and images, and access to the relevant systems where needed. We will agree the exact inputs before starting.

Do you guarantee search rankings or sales?+

No. Search rankings, AI citations, traffic and sales depend on factors beyond a service provider’s control. We agree concrete deliverables instead of promising business results.

Do you provide custom quotations?+

Yes. If a package does not fit, tell us about your project. Larger websites, custom applications, mobile app development and ongoing services are scoped individually.

Where is the company registered?+

ORBITIT SOLUTION LIMITED is incorporated in England and Wales, company number 17341168. The registered office is shown in our footer and company profile. It is not described as a staffed visitor office.
