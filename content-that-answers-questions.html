CONTENT & DISCOVERY

Build content around the questions your customers ask

Useful service pages help readers understand the work, the limits and the next step without making inflated promises.

OrbitIT editorial · Practical guidance

Use the customer’s question

Describe what a service does in direct language. Explain who it is suitable for, what is delivered and what information is needed to start.

Make structure do the work

Use descriptive headings, short paragraphs and meaningful links. Put closely related content together so readers can move from a broad service description to a detailed package.

Keep business facts consistent

Use the same legal entity name, contact channels and registration details across your public business information. Distinguish a registered address from an operating or visitor location.

Avoid promises you cannot prove

Do not publish invented customer feedback, demand claims or guaranteed search outcomes. A clear process and verifiable deliverables provide a more useful basis for a conversation.
