WEB STRATEGY

Before the first pixel: planning a useful business website

A short brief can save a lot of uncertainty. Start with your audience, content and the action you want visitors to take.

OrbitIT editorial · Practical guidance

Start with one useful outcome

A business website needs a clear purpose. Decide whether the primary action is a phone call, an enquiry, a booking or a product purchase. Your homepage should make that next step easy to find.

Map the pages before the design

List the essential questions customers ask: what you do, who is behind the business, how the service works and how to get in touch. Turn those questions into a small, purposeful page structure.

Gather approved content

Collect accurate service descriptions, brand files and images you have permission to use. Legal company details and contact information should be consistent across the site.

Agree the handover

Clarify who owns the domain, hosting, content and source files. Decide how enquiries will be delivered and who will maintain the site after launch. A launch checklist is as valuable as the visual design.
