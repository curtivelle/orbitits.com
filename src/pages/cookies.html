Frontend storage

The shipped frontend does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not use local storage to retain enquiry text. Service filters and package selections work within the current page or through URL parameters.

Enquiry security session

The active WhatsApp form does not call the server contact endpoint or set its session cookie. If optional server delivery is enabled in a future integration, a temporary session cookie supports request verification and basic rate limiting. It is configured as HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax and Secure when HTTPS is in use. Enquiry content is not stored in that session.

Clipboard and email apps

Copying an enquiry writes text to your device clipboard at your request. Opening your email app hands the prepared text to your configured application; that application and provider have their own privacy settings.

External services

The site does not embed maps, videos or social widgets. The WhatsApp enquiry link passes the prepared message to WhatsApp when you choose to open it; review and send it within that service. Following external links may take you to services with their own cookie practices. If analytics or other optional technologies are added later, this policy and any necessary controls must be updated before they are used.
