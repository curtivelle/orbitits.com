Service & Refund Policy

How scope, delivery, changes, cancellation and support are handled.

Delivery and starting work

Services are delivered against an agreed written brief. The delivery timetable starts as agreed after necessary materials, access and any agreed payment are available. Timings are confirmed per project rather than guaranteed by a package listing.

Revisions and additional work

The included revision rounds and deliverables are stated in the package or quotation. Feedback should be consolidated. New requirements and work beyond the agreed limits need a separate written approval and, where applicable, an additional quotation.

Cancellation requests

Contact the business email with your name, project reference, request and the relevant dates. Cancellation is assessed against the agreed project terms, work already performed, any external commitments and applicable rights. There is no blanket no-refund rule.

Consumers and statutory rights

Where UK consumer distance-contract rules apply to a service, a cancellation period generally runs for 14 days from contract conclusion. Any request to begin within that period, proportionate charges for work performed, or loss of cancellation rights on full performance must follow the applicable requirements. Nothing here removes mandatory consumer rights. See the Consumer Contracts Regulations.

Refund review and payment

We will review the request and explain the outcome and basis. Where a refund is due, the amount and processing arrangements will be communicated, subject to any statutory deadlines. Payment-provider processing may affect when cleared funds appear.

Faults, handover and support

Report an issue with enough information to reproduce it. Correction responsibilities, handover and any support period depend on the agreed scope and applicable rights. Ongoing maintenance or platform management is not included unless explicitly agreed.

Complaints

Send the project reference, issue and requested resolution to the contact above. We will review the information and discuss an appropriate next step. These website policies do not replace agreed project-specific terms or statutory protections.
